Overview

Positano is a cliffside village on southern Italy's Amalfi Coast. It's a well-known holiday destination with a pebble beachfront and steep, narrow streets lined with boutiques and cafes. Its Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta features a majolica-tiled dome and a 13th-century Byzantine icon of the Virgin Mary.

Beyond the scenic views, Positano offers a gateway to the "Sentiero degli Dei" (Path of the Gods), a hiking trail that provides spectacular aerial views of the entire coastline. The village itself is a masterpiece of urban planning, where every level of stairs leads to a new discovery of Mediterranean flavors and artisanal crafts.
